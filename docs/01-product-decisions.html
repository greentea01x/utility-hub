Product summary

Elevator pitch

Juristic Platform is a web application that lets an apartment's juristic person manage the three charges a tenancy actually generates every month — water, electricity and the common area fee — without touching rent. Staff walk the building with a phone and type each room's water and electricity meter readings; the system subtracts last month's reading to get the units used and prices them automatically, then calculates the common area fee from the room's floor area multiplied by the rate per square metre. The three charges are combined into a monthly bill for the juristic person to review and approve, after which the bill is delivered to the tenant through the apartment's LINE Official Account. When the tenant brings cash to the office, the counter confirms receipt in one action: the bill flips to Paid, a numbered receipt is issued and printed, and a confirmation message goes back out on LINE. A dashboard and four reports show what was billed, what was collected, who is in arrears and how much water and electricity each room has used over the past twelve months.

Adapted from PRD §29, in English, for the documentation set. The Thai wording the team speaks aloud stays as written in the PRD.

Actors & roles

Admin / นิติบุคคล admin

The juristic person's office. Works on a desktop browser, occasionally on a phone. Owns every money action: rates, approval, cash, receipts, voids, announcements, reports and settings. Also operates the cash counter — PRD §12's sentence giving that to เจ้าหน้าที่ is a drafting error corrected by D-36.

Staff / เจ้าหน้าที่ · ช่าง staff

Walks the building with a phone and records meter readings, including a meter replacement when a meter has been changed. Sees room counts and reading progress and nothing else — no amount, no rate, no payment, no receipt, no tenant record. Money is absent from the API responses a staff session receives, not merely hidden in the navigation.

Tenant / ผู้เช่า · ลูกบ้าน no login

Never opens the web application and installs nothing. Adds the apartment's LINE Official Account as a friend and sends a one-time code to bind their room, then receives the bill, the reminders, the payment confirmation and building announcements. A tenant with no binding is handed a printed A5 ใบแจ้งค่าใช้จ่าย at the office.

What each stage guarantees

  • Reading round — a room is จดแล้ว only when both utilities are saved; units can never be negative; the previous value is resolved by greatest earlier period, or the room's opening baseline. (D-15, D-13, D-14, D-06)
  • Generation — idempotent on UNIQUE(room_id, period); re-running creates only missing bills and recalculates Draft ones; approved bills are never touched; every bill carries exactly three line items. (D-18, D-23, D-41)
  • Approval — one gate, Admin only; freezes readings, units, rates, area, tenant name, amounts and the due date; commits in one fast transaction and drains the LINE queue afterwards. (D-19, D-20, D-22)
  • Delivery — never blocks approval; one notifications row per recipient with a retry counter; an unlinked tenant is a badge and a printed bill. (D-30, D-28)
  • Counter — full amount only, one live payment per bill, enforced by a unique index and a conditional update, with the receipt number allocated inside the same transaction. (D-24, D-26)
  • Correction — the only way back is Admin reverting a room's most recent bill to Draft with a written reason; a Paid bill is corrected only after the cash is returned and the payment voided. (D-22, D-25)

The five most consequential

D-11 · Meter readings

A billing period is the billing month, a plain <code>YYYY-MM</code> string on readings and bills. There is no periods table, no open/close action and no closed state anywhere in the product — a reading is editable exactly while its room's bill for that period is Draft or absent.

Why it is load-bearing: Billing-month periods are the only reading that reproduces the PRD §28 demo exactly. Dropping the open/close state machine removes a table, two Admin actions and a whole class of "period is closed" bugs, because the bill status already answers every question the lock would have answered — and a derived completeness line gives the operator the signal the lock was standing in for.

D-21 · Billing & statuses

Overdue is derived from the due date, never stored. <code>bills.status</code> holds only Draft, Unpaid and Paid; one SQL expression and one JS helper produce the fourth display value, and there is no late fee, interest or penalty of any kind.

Why it is load-bearing: A stored status that depends on a cron job on free-tier hosting reads as a broken feature the moment the instance sleeps; a derived status is always correct, survives a demo on a laptop, and makes the expected value in every test deterministic. Stating the rule as a display rule rather than an absolute ban keeps it from being ignored where it matters.

D-22 · Billing & statuses

Approval freezes the bill. The single correction path is Admin reverting a room's most recent bill to Draft with a written reason; a Paid bill is corrected only after the cash goes back across the counter and the payment is voided. The system never holds a refund, credit or balance.

Why it is load-bearing: One reversible path covers both cases, keeps exactly four statuses, and makes the write guard a single check on bill.status in the middleware. Restricting the revert to the newest bill is the one rule that keeps the previous-reading chain honest, and routing the refund across the counter is what lets the system stay free of money it does not hold.

D-30 · LINE & notifications

Money state and messaging state are independent. Bills commit first and send afterwards, every attempt is one row per recipient with a retry counter, and an unlinked or failed tenant is a badge and a printable paper bill — never a blocked approval.

Why it is load-bearing: On day one almost no tenant is linked, so the unlinked case is the common case. Recording delivery per recipient is what makes ส่งบิลผ่าน LINE OA verifiable, gives the demo an honest failure path, and lets one table serve bills and announcements; an UPSERT with a counter is what makes the retry policy the decision already promised actually expressible.

D-44 · Platform

The seed computes every date from the run date. PRD §28's calendar values (5 ก.ย. 2026, 3 ก.ย. 2026, REC-202609-000852) are documentation and mockup values; the live demo reproduces the arithmetic — 10 หน่วย, 70 หน่วย, 180, 560, 1,050, 1,790 บาท — never the calendar.

Why it is load-bearing: The demo is the deliverable that gets graded, so its data is a product decision. Relative dates are the only way the graded demo shows a payable bill instead of an overdue one with a wrong receipt number; a uniform 30 ตร.ม. block makes the PRD's own headline Dashboard number come out exactly right without an examiner asking why vacant rooms are larger; and stating plainly which PRD figures are arithmetically impossible stops three documents being written against numbers the system can never produce.

Assumptions

Each of these is a statement about the world that the decisions above rely on. If one turns out to be false, the decisions it supports have to be revisited — that is the point of writing them down rather than leaving them implicit.

  2. PRD §12's sentence granting Confirm Cash Payment to เจ้าหน้าที่ contradicts §4 and is treated as a drafting error; §4 wins (D-36). This is the single place where a decision overrides literal PRD text, and it must be flagged in the document set.
  3. PRD §16's ยอดค่าน้ำ 18,500 and ยอดค่าไฟ 62,500, and PRD §19's 35 ตร.ม. example room, are illustrative and not reproducible at the stated rates and seed; only the room counts and the 70,350 common area total are treated as reproducible (D-44).

  5. 'UtilityHub' at the top of the PRD is superseded by 'Juristic platform' at the bottom and by the existing template, repo and directory names; it is deleted rather than reconciled (D-01).

  11. Free-tier hosting sleeps and restarts; no scheduled work may live inside the Node process, no state may live in process memory, and staying awake requires its own 10-minute ping (D-32, D-37, D-43).
  12. Approval of a period's bills happens within a few days of the reading round, which is what makes the MAX(due day, approval + 3 days) rule produce the office's usual due date rather than a shifted one (D-20).
  13. Tenants pay in cash at the office during posted office hours; a tenant who cannot pay in full returns later — the counter never takes a part payment, and a refund after a voided payment is handed over in cash at the counter, never recorded as a system balance (D-24, D-25).

  15. The team is 3-4 students over one semester with no dedicated QA, so every decision above was chosen to be implementable and testable by the same person who designed it.
  16. Thai names, phone numbers and all seed content are fictional; no real tenant data enters the repository or the demo database.

  1. Choose the month. Open จดมิเตอร์ or บิลประจำเดือน and set the month picker; it defaults to the current calendar month in Asia/Bangkok. There is no "open period" action, no lock and no closed state — a billing period is just the month string the readings and the bills carry (D-11).
  2. Run the reading round. Staff (or Admin) walk the floors on จดมิเตอร์ with the ยังไม่จด filter, one card per room, water and electricity, บันทึก per room. A room counts as จดแล้ว only when both utilities are saved; the sticky bar reads จดแล้ว {x}/{y} ห้อง and the round is finished when the two numbers match. Vacant rooms are not in that count and are never billed, though a voluntary reading may still be recorded for them under the ห้องว่าง filter (D-15, D-07). A current reading below the previous one is blocked outright unless เปลี่ยนมิเตอร์ในรอบนี้ is ticked and the replacement fields filled in (D-13).
  3. Review the round before generating. Open /readings?view=review for the same month. Look at three things: the rooms still ยังไม่จด (they will produce no bill and appear in the run summary as ยังไม่ได้จด), the ใช้สูงผิดปกติ flags, and every meter replacement recorded on the round with its Thai reason. Correcting a reading now costs one save; correcting it after approval costs a revert with a written reason (D-13, D-22).
  4. Generate the drafts. Press ส่งให้นิติตรวจสอบ on จดมิเตอร์, or สร้าง/อัปเดตบิลฉบับร่าง on บิลประจำเดือน. The run is safe to repeat: it creates a Draft bill for every billable room that has both readings and no bill yet, recalculates the Draft bills that already exist, and never touches an approved one. Read the summary line it returns — สร้างบิลใหม่ {a} ใบ · อัปเดต {b} ใบ · ข้าม (อนุมัติแล้ว) {c} ใบ · ยังไม่ได้จด {d} ห้อง — and go back to step 2 if {d} is not zero (D-18, D-23).
  5. Review the drafts. On บิลประจำเดือน the completeness line reads บิลครบทุกห้อง {billed}/{billable} · อนุมัติแล้ว {approved} · ชำระแล้ว {paid}. Open any flagged room on รายละเอียดบิล. Editing a reading now recalculates that Draft bill immediately on save — this is the last free moment (D-12, D-11).
  6. Approve & send. Admin only. Tick the rows (or select-all-in-view) and press อนุมัติและส่งบิล ({N} ห้อง); the dialog says อนุมัติและส่งบิล {N} ห้อง ผ่าน LINE — ยกเลิกไม่ได้. Approval freezes readings, units, unit prices, room area, tenant name, every line amount, the total and the due date. Delivery happens afterwards and can never block or undo the approval; the result summary lists the rooms that received nothing, and those get paper (procedure f) (D-19, D-22, D-30).
  7. Take the cash and print the receipts as tenants come to the counter over the following days — procedure b. Meanwhile the daily job at 09:00 sends the reminder ladder by itself: PRE_DUE two days before the due date, then OVERDUE on day 1, day 8 and day 15 past it, and nothing after that (D-33, D-32).
  8. Close the month on รายงาน. สรุปการเรียกเก็บและรับชำระรายเดือน for the period's billed, collected and outstanding split by utility; รายการค้างชำระ aged 1-7 / 8-30 / 31-60 / 61+ วัน for the phone calls; each with a CSV export (D-42, D-41).
The calendar rule for the due date — read this once

ตั้งค่า holds วันครบกำหนด as a day of the month, default 5, Admin-editable, range 1–28. A Draft bill displays กำหนดชำระ {day} of its own period month, labelled (ยืนยันเมื่ออนุมัติ). At Approve & Send the date is computed once and frozen:

due_date = MAX(day-of-month of the bill's own period month, approval date + 3 days)

Approve the September bills on 1 Sep with due day 5 and every one of them is due 5 ก.ย. Approve them late, on the 4th, and they are due the 7th — the tenant always gets three days, and that is correct behaviour rather than a defect. Every bill approved in the same batch on the same day carries the same due date, the day is clamped to the last day of a short month, and there is no per-bill due-date field anywhere: it is never editable by hand (D-20). A bill becomes เกินกำหนด by itself at 00:00 the day after — no job runs, no status is stored, and no late fee, interest or penalty of any kind is ever added (D-21).

b · The counter — cash, receipt, void and reprint

Screens: รับชำระเงิน /payments · ประวัติการรับชำระ /payments/history · ใบเสร็จ /receipts/:id · รายละเอียดบิล /bills/:id. Who: Admin only — รับชำระเงิน and ออกใบเสร็จ are Admin actions, and Staff cannot reach the screen at all (D-36). Before: the bill is รอชำระ or เกินกำหนด. After: the bill is ชำระแล้ว, one numbered receipt exists, and the tenant has had a LINE confirmation if they are linked.

  1. Find the bill. รับชำระเงิน, search by room. A ฉบับร่าง bill cannot be paid — it has not been approved — and a ชำระแล้ว bill has no confirm button.
  2. Count the cash against the screen. ยอดที่ต้องชำระ 1,790.00 บาท is read-only text, not a field: the amount is the bill total and cannot be typed over. The รับเงิน box beside the button is an optional aid that computes เงินทอน live — รับเงิน 2,000 → เงินทอน 210.00 — and neither number is stored, printed on the receipt or sent anywhere (D-24).
  3. Refuse a partial payment. There is no field to enter a smaller amount into, and this is deliberate. A tenant who is short is asked to come back with the full amount; nothing is recorded, the bill stays รอชำระ or เกินกำหนด and keeps its due date. Do not take part of the money and hold it — the system has no place to record a balance, no credit note and no refund, and the only way to undo a payment is to void it and hand the whole sum back (D-24, D-25, D-05 limitation 2).
  4. Confirm. ยืนยันรับเงินสด. One press does everything in one transaction: writes the payment against your own login, flips the bill to ชำระแล้ว, allocates the receipt number and queues the LINE confirmation. A double-click is harmless — the second attempt loses the race in the database and answers บิลนี้ถูกชำระไปแล้ว, and two cashiers on one bill produce one payment and one receipt number (D-24, D-26).
  5. Print the receipt. The receipt opens on /receipts/:id; press พิมพ์ใบเสร็จ, which prints A5 portrait through the browser's own dialog. It carries the letterhead, เลขที่ REC-202609-000852, วันที่ชำระ, ห้อง 205, ชื่อผู้เช่า, รอบบิล, the three line items, ยอดรวม 1,790.00 บาท with the amount in Thai words, ชำระโดย เงินสด, your own ชื่อที่แสดง as ผู้รับเงิน and a signature line. It is a non-tax ใบเสร็จรับเงิน — no VAT, no ใบกำกับภาษี (D-27, D-28).
  6. Reprint. Always use the button, never the browser's own File → Print. The first button-driven print is the original; from then on the button reads พิมพ์สำเนา and every sheet is marked สำเนา. Later reprints are found on ประวัติการรับชำระ — search by room code or by receipt number, then พิมพ์สำเนา. The number never changes on a reprint. Printing from the browser menu bypasses the counter and produces an unmarked copy, which is a listed limitation rather than a bug (D-27, D-05 limitation 11).
  7. Void a mistaken payment — wrong room, wrong month, wrong bill. Admin only, from รายละเอียดบิล or from the receipt: ยกเลิกการชำระเงิน with a Thai reason of at least 10 characters. Hand the cash back across the counter first — the dialog says so in as many words: คืนเงินสด {amount} บาท ให้ผู้เช่าก่อนยืนยันการยกเลิก (D-25, D-39).
  8. Know what the void does to the receipt number. Nothing is deleted. The payment row is marked voided and kept; the receipt row is marked voided and kept, so it keeps its number, and every view and every print of it from then on is stamped ยกเลิก in red across the header. Keeping the number is the entire point of voiding rather than deleting: the evidence that money was taken and returned survives. The bill goes back to รอชำระ and displays as เกินกำหนด by itself if the due date has passed; no LINE message is sent on the void, so the office explains in person; and the bill is excluded from the automatic PRE_DUE and OVERDUE sends and badged ระงับการแจ้งเตือนอัตโนมัติ so nobody chases a tenant who did nothing wrong. Taking the money again is an ordinary payment and issues a new receipt with a new number (D-25, D-27, D-33).
Receipt numbers, and why a gap is not a mistake

Numbers are REC-<payment month YYYYMM>-<6 digits> from one counter that never resets. They are unique and always increasing, but they are not gapless — an abandoned transaction and the seed's starting offset both leave holes legitimately. Two things are guaranteed and are the only two worth checking: no two receipts share a number, and a later payment never gets a lower one (D-26, D-27, D-05 limitation 10).

c · Tenant move-in and the LINE link-code slip

Screens: ผู้เช่า /tenants · the printable slip /tenants/:id/link-code/slip. Who: Admin only — Staff cannot see the Tenants page (D-36). Before: the room is ว่าง. After: the room is มีผู้เช่า, and the tenant is holding a slip that turns into a LINE binding the moment they use it.

  1. Check the room is free. One room has at most one Active tenant, enforced in the database — a second one is refused with ห้องนี้มีผู้เช่าอยู่แล้ว กรุณาสิ้นสุดสัญญาเช่าเดิมก่อน. If the previous tenant is still Active, run procedure d first (D-10).
  2. Create the tenant record. ผู้เช่า → new: ชื่อ-นามสกุล, เบอร์โทรศัพท์ in the Thai mobile form 0XXXXXXXXX, ห้อง, วันที่เข้าอยู่. LINE User ID is not a field you type — it is filled only by the linking flow below. Saving sets the room to มีผู้เช่า in the same transaction (D-10, D-07).
  3. Do not touch the meter baselines. เลขมิเตอร์เริ่มต้น belongs to the room, not to the tenant. A re-let room already has the departing tenant's final reading sitting there as its previous value, and the ordinary lookup finds it — re-entering anything here double-bills someone. Baselines are Admin-only and editable only while the room has no reading at all (D-06, D-14).
  4. Create the link code. Press สร้างรหัสผูก LINE on the tenant's row. It is six characters from an alphabet with no O, 0, I or 1, valid 7 days, single use (D-29).
  5. Print the slip from /tenants/:id/link-code/slip — A5, carrying the code and the Official Account QR — and hand it over with the keys.
  6. Tell the tenant the two steps on the slip: add the Official Account as a friend, then send the six characters as an ordinary chat message. They immediately get back ✅ ผูกบัญชีสำเร็จ · คุณจะได้รับบิลและประกาศของห้อง {room_code} ผ่าน LINE นี้. A wrong or expired code answers ❌ ไม่พบรหัสนี้ หรือรหัสหมดอายุแล้ว กรุณาติดต่อสำนักงานนิติบุคคล — issue a fresh code; never type a user id into the system by hand, because there is no field for it (D-29).
  7. Confirm the binding. ผู้เช่า then shows the LINE display name and the linked date, and offers ยกเลิกการผูก. Until the tenant links, they are billed exactly as normal and handed paper — procedure f. One LINE account may legitimately be bound to several rooms (a parent renting two, a couple sharing a phone); every message names its room, and such an account receives one message per room (D-29, D-30).

d · Tenant move-out — the order of operations

Screens: จดมิเตอร์ /readings · บิลประจำเดือน /bills · รับชำระเงิน /payments · ผู้เช่า /tenants. Who: Admin only. Before: the tenant is Active and their last month is unread and unbilled. After: the tenant is Inactive with a settled final bill, the LINE binding is cleared and the room is ว่าง and ready to re-let. This order is not advisory — the system refuses the last step until the ones before it are done (D-08).

The two conditions that block สิ้นสุดสัญญาเช่า

The action is BLOCKED unless both hold: (a) a final reading exists for both utilities for that room, dated on or after the last period the room was billed for; and (b) the bill covering that reading exists and is Paid. Ending a tenancy is a settlement, not a status flip — without this gate the departing tenant's last month is skipped by the run and silently charged to whoever moves in next (D-08).

  1. Record the final reading — water and electricity — for that room on จดมิเตอร์, in the current period (D-08, D-12).
  2. Generate that room's bill. It is created in the current period like any other. Nothing is prorated in any direction: the tenant whose tenancy is Active on the generation date receives the entire period's water, electricity and common area fee, and there is no off-cycle settlement bill (D-08, D-18).
  3. Approve and send it. The tenant is still bound to LINE at this point, so they actually receive it (D-19).
  4. Take the cash at the counter and issue the receipt — procedure b. The bill must reach Paid; a partial payment does not settle a tenancy any more than it settles a bill (D-24).
  5. Only now press สิ้นสุดสัญญาเช่า on ผู้เช่า. One transaction does all four things: the tenant becomes Inactive, วันที่ย้ายออก is written as the date you pressed it, the LINE binding is cleared, and the room becomes ว่าง. The binding is cleared at this step and not before, which is why the departing tenant still got both their final bill and their payment confirmation (D-08, D-10).
  6. Check what survived. The outgoing tenant's bills, payments and receipts stay attached to them forever and remain visible in the room's 12-month history; the tenant record is never deleted. Their final reading is the next tenant's previous reading through the ordinary lookup, so no consumption crosses a tenancy, nothing is re-entered and nothing is billed twice (D-42, D-14).

วันที่เข้าอยู่ and วันที่ย้ายออก are shown on the Tenants page and in the per-room history, but neither date influences any amount, neither is future-dated, and วันที่ย้ายออก is written by the action rather than typed (D-08, D-10).

e · Changing a rate, and which period it hits

Screen: ตั้งค่า /settings. Who: Admin only — Staff never see a rate (D-36). Before: know which billing period you intend the new price to apply to. After: the old rate is intact as history, and every bill in a period carries one rate no matter how often generation is re-run.

  1. Enter the new อัตรา and its วันที่มีผล. The effective date defaults to today and may be set in the future. Saving inserts a new row and never overwrites the old one, so rate history is complete and auditable; a second rate for the same utility on the same date is refused with มีอัตราของวันที่นี้อยู่แล้ว (D-17).
  2. Work out which period it hits — this is the step people get wrong. Generation picks, per utility, the rate whose วันที่มีผล is the greatest date on or before the FIRST DAY of the bill's period month. So a rate effective 15 ก.ย. 2026 does not touch the September bills at all — 1 September is earlier than it — and first applies in October. To change September, the effective date must be 1 ก.ย. or earlier (D-17).
  3. Expect approved bills not to move. The unit price is snapshotted onto the bill item at generation, and every screen, print view and LINE message renders from that snapshot. A rate change can never rewrite a bill that has been approved, sent or paid (D-17, D-22).
  4. Deal with Draft bills the change now covers. If Draft bills exist for a period a back-dated rate reaches, ตั้งค่า shows มีบิลฉบับร่าง {N} ใบในรอบนี้ ยังใช้อัตราเดิมอยู่ with the button คำนวณบิลฉบับร่างใหม่. Press it — it recalculates Draft bills only and cannot touch anything else (D-17).
  5. Tell the tenants yourself if it matters. A rate change sends no message. Use ประกาศ if the building should hear about it before the bills arrive (D-35).

Room area works the same way and for the same reason: it is snapshotted onto the bill item, so correcting an area never rewrites a receipt already issued (D-09).

f · When a tenant has no LINE — the paper path

Screens: บิลประจำเดือน /bills · รายละเอียดบิล /bills/:id. Before: the bill is approved and the delivery badge reads ยังไม่ได้ส่ง — ไม่มี LINE. After: the tenant is holding an A5 ใบแจ้งค่าใช้จ่าย carrying the same three lines and the same due date as the LINE message would have.

  1. Expect it at approval, do not be surprised by it. The Approve & Send result summary counts ยังไม่ได้ส่ง (ไม่มี LINE) {y} and lists the rooms; the same badge appears on the bill's row and on รายละเอียดบิล. Approval is never blocked by delivery — money state and messaging state are independent (D-30, D-19).
  2. Print the paper bill. Open each such room's รายละเอียดบิล and press พิมพ์ใบแจ้งหนี้. That is the A5 ใบแจ้งค่าใช้จ่าย: the same layout as the receipt with the letterhead, ห้อง, ชื่อผู้เช่า, รอบบิล, กำหนดชำระ and the three line items with units, rate and amount — and deliberately no receipt number, no payment date, no ผู้รับเงิน and no signature line, because nothing has been paid yet. It is available for any bill that is not ฉบับร่าง (D-28).
  3. Deliver it on paper — under the door or at the counter — and note that this tenant will get no automatic reminder either, because PRE_DUE and OVERDUE are LINE messages. Chase them by phone; เบอร์โทรศัพท์ is on the tenant record and in the รายการค้างชำระ report (D-33, D-42).
  4. Fix the cause, not just this month. Issue a link code and a slip — procedure c. The paper path is a fallback, not a plan (D-29).
ส่งแล้ว is not proof the tenant read it

If a tenant has blocked the Official Account, LINE accepts the message, reports success and drops it. The system cannot tell that apart from a delivered message and the word "blocked" appears nowhere in the product for exactly that reason. A tenant who insists they were never told, whose badge says ส่งแล้ว, is handled the same way as one with no binding: print the paper bill and hand it over (D-30, D-05 limitation 9).

Documentation values vs. live demo values

เตรียมสาธิต — the two preparations that happen before anyone is in the room

Step 0 · Arm the demo — npm run db:demo-arm

The seed ships the finished current period: room 205 is already read and already carries its ฉบับร่าง bill, which is what every screen figure, fixture id and test in this document set assumes. On an untouched npm run db:reset the จดมิเตอร์ list therefore shows room 205 as จดแล้ว and pressing ส่งให้นิติตรวจสอบ reports สร้างบิลใหม่ 0 ใบ · อัปเดต 1 ใบ — actions 1 and 2 below would have nothing to do. npm run db:demo-arm applies db/demo-reset.sql, which deletes room 205's current-period readings and bill and resets the receipt counter to 851, and touches nothing else — not the other 66 bills, not the settings row. It is idempotent, it takes about a second, and it is run again after any practice run (engineering specification §2.7).

เตรียมสาธิต LINE — three steps, or nothing is delivered

On a fresh seed the LINE half of the demo cannot work, in either mode. Room 205's tenant carries the placeholder id Useed0000…0037, which is never a valid push target (D-44), and line_test_user_id ships empty, which is the swallow branch: with โหมดทดสอบ LINE ON no API call is made at all and the delivery badge still turns ส่งแล้ว; with it OFF the push is attempted against the Useed… id, LINE answers 400 and the badge turns ส่งไม่สำเร็จ. Neither puts a message on a phone. Do this before the examiner arrives (engineering specification §5.8.5):

  1. Give the demo a real recipient. Either open ผู้เช่า, press สร้างรหัสผูก LINE for room 205's tenant and send that 6-character code from the team's own LINE account — which overwrites the Useed… id with a genuine one (D-29) — or, more safely, set บัญชีรับข้อความทดสอบ on ตั้งค่า to the team account and leave โหมดทดสอบ LINE ON, which redirects every message to that one account while still recording the intended recipient (D-34).
  2. Read the mode back on ตั้งค่า. โหมดทดสอบ LINE: เปิด · บัญชีรับข้อความทดสอบ: ตั้งค่าแล้ว. Never trust the default — the whole hazard is that a wrong configuration looks identical on the badges.
  3. Send one test bill. Run step 0, walk room 205 through จดมิเตอร์ → ส่งให้นิติตรวจสอบ → อนุมัติและส่งบิล once, and watch it land on the phone. Then run step 0 again to re-arm the room for the real demo.

The demo sends to two or three real accounts and never bulk-approves 67 rooms live: at three followers the free plan's 500 monthly messages are inexhaustible, at 67 they are not (D-34).

Rehearsal safety

โหมดทดสอบ LINE defaults to ON, redirecting every outbound message to one whitelisted account while still recording the intended recipient. It is switched off only for the rehearsed demo path, which sends to the two or three real accounts the team owns — that is what keeps a 67-room rehearsal from spending the free plan's 500-message monthly allowance and from unbinding the seeded tenants (D-34, D-44). The safer configuration, and the one this document recommends, is to leave test mode ON and point บัญชีรับข้อความทดสอบ at the team account: the redirect is then the safety net rather than something switched off under pressure.

After every rehearsal, run npm run db:demo-arm again. A rehearsal leaves room 205 read, billed, approved and paid, and the next run has to start from the same place this one did.
