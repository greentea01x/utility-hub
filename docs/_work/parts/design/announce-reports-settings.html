Announcements, Reports & Settings

Three Admin-only surfaces that sit outside the billing spine: the one place the office speaks to every tenant at once (Announcements), the four reports the office opens when someone disputes a number (Reports), and the two pages that decide what every future bill will cost and who may press the buttons (Settings and the Users page it links to). All four screens are desktop-only: D-45 commits the phone surface to Login, จดมิเตอร์ and the reduced ภาพรวม, and D-36 keeps every screen in this section out of the Staff role entirely — so there is no 390 px variant to design, and the only phone-shaped artefact here is the LINE bubble preview, which is a picture of the tenant's phone, not a page of ours.

Decisions this section renders, and the four assumptions it adds

Rendered verbatim: D-35 (audience is two options only — ทุกห้องที่มีผู้เช่า or เลือกชั้น; live preview; live room + linked count; immutable history), D-31 §6 (the ANNOUNCEMENT template), D-34 (multicast for announcements, the 500-message line, LINE test mode default ON), D-39 §4 (the one confirmation dialog on this section's only irreversible action), D-42 (exactly four reports, each with a CSV export), D-41 (money figures read approved bills), D-21 (Overdue is derived, never stored), D-17 (insert-only effective-dated rates plus the Draft-recalculation notice), D-20 (due day 1–28, and the MAX(day, approval + 3) rule), D-33 (the reminder schedule), D-32 (the manual job button and its result line), D-46 (the Users field list, Inactive instead of delete, ตั้งรหัสผ่านใหม่ as the only recovery path), D-40 (403, empty, failed-save, loading), D-03 (formats), D-44 (every figure below reconciles against the seed).

Assumption A-D1 — routes. The ADR fixes page routes as lowercase, plural, hyphenated and puts reports/*.ejs in its own view folder, but names no report path. This section uses /announcements, /reports/monthly, /reports/arrears, /reports/top-usage, /reports/room-history, /settings, /users, with /reports redirecting to /reports/monthly. Each CSV is the same path plus .csv, so the export button is an ordinary link and needs no eighth JSON endpoint.

Assumption A-D2 — export filenames. D-42 fixes report-{name}-{period}.csv. Two reports are not single-period: the monthly summary writes the range as report-monthly-2026-04_2026-09.csv, and the per-room history names the room, report-room-history-205-2026-09.csv. Both keep the prefix, the separator and the extension the helper already produces.

Assumption A-D3 — reminder days are shown, not edited. The brief asks for reminder days in Settings; D-33 fixes them as constants (due − 2, then due + 1, 8, 15, all at 09:00 Asia/Bangkok). Settings therefore renders the schedule read-only next to the manual trigger of D-32. Making it editable would create a setting no test asserts and a fifth message nobody decided to send.

Assumption A-D4 — no receipt-footer field. The brief asks for a receipt footer; the settings table has no such column and D-27 fixes the receipt's fields exactly. Settings instead renders a live letterhead preview of the four fields that do print (name, address, optional tax ID, phone), so the Admin can see the effect of an edit without a free-text field that would let a VAT line back onto a non-tax receipt.

One deviation from the brief, on purpose: the brief lists an audience of “all rooms / selected rooms / floor”. D-35 allows exactly two options and forbids per-room selection. The decision log wins.
